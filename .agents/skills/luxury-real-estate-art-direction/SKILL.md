# Luxury Real Estate Art Direction

Use this skill when defining the brand look, creating section compositions, or visually polishing the landing page.

## Art direction
- Concept: **SOLEN Residences** (replaceable placeholder brand), editorial architecture magazine meets luxury property campaign.
- Mood: serene, tactile, sunlit stone and glass, lush trees, softly shadowed interiors, confident and quiet rather than flashy.
- Palette: pine `#12231F`, dark ink `#17211F`, ivory `#F7F4EC`, linen `#E9E5D9`, brushed brass `#B69767`, muted slate `#71807A`.
- Font pairing: expressive editorial serif headings (e.g. Cormorant Garamond) with modern sans-serif body (e.g. Manrope); choose accessible fallbacks.
- Use oversized headlines, 12-column desktop rhythm, fluid type, alternating bright/dark chapters, hairline rules, small uppercase labels, considered spacing.
- Images should depict coherent contemporary residences: warm minimal interiors, sculptural staircases, verdant courtyards, understated façades, golden-hour terraces. Favor assets supplied by user; otherwise use permitted, creditable image sources with reliable fallback placeholders.

## Section framing
1. Hero with immersive full-bleed architectural photography (or optional poster + muted looping video), cinematic title, short proposition, discovery CTA and subtle scroll cue.
2. Editorial introduction: one memorable statement and short description.
3. Three featured property presentations, each with generous image area, location, size/property type, and a detail affordance.
4. Signature architecture chapter with split-screen photo and immersive line of copy.
5. Lifestyle/neighborhood image story; do not invent measured travel distances or statistics.
6. Strong closing inquiry chapter and minimalist footer.

## Visual quality gate
- Each section should contribute a distinct visual beat, not look like a copy-pasted card grid.
- Keep primary CTA visible and headlines highly legible on images; use deliberate gradient/overlay where needed.
- Avoid false prestige claims, stock watermark assets, fake ratings, fabricated awards or unverified data.
- Check 1440px, 768px and 390px layouts: no awkward wrapping, overflow, cropped faces or decorative overlaps blocking content.
